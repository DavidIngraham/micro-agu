Controls & model

Right control: left/right steering. In MANUAL, pull down for both brakes. Arrow keys do the same. The vertical slider and W/S control throttle in MANUAL/FBWA. In FBWB/CRUISE it commands climb or descent and springs to altitude hold on release. Left/right also nudges AUTO/LOITER; release returns steering to the autopilot.

MANUAL directly controls brakes and throttle. FBWA uses heading-rate steering with manual throttle. FBWB controls altitude; CRUISE also holds heading; click the altitude display to set its altitude target.

Starts held on the ground while sensors initialize. The default circuit uploads and starts automatically in AUTO when sensors are ready. Click the mode display to take over. Reset starts a fresh session.

Full 9-DOF canopy–payload research model, compiled from Rust. Both bodies, rotor and brake actuators are drawn from simulation truth. Uses the as-built 132 mm riser spacing and a fit to existing Hood River / Trout Lake logs. Ground contact uses a flat plane; inverted setup, overhead swing, two steps and toss use an assisted trajectory, followed by ArduPlane TAKEOFF climb-out. Canopy motion and inflation during handling are prescribed approximations. Launch timing and friction are assumptions, not newly fitted measurements. All control is local to this tab.
