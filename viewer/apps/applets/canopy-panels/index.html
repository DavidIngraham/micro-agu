INSIDE THE SIMULATION

One canopy. Five aerodynamic regions.

Curvature changes each region’s orientation. Local airflow and brake pull set its lift and drag.

Five equal-area integration regions, not five articulated bodies. Main-view force arrows share a scale of 0.06 m/N; blue airflow arrows use 0.06 s. Dashed lines show moment arms from the CG. Panel numbers run aircraft left (1) to aircraft right (5); these appear reversed when viewed from the front.

2 · Inspect a local panel

Airflow is opposite the panel’s velocity through the air. Rotation of the canopy adds local velocity at each panel’s offset. These two snapshots have zero angular rate.

3 · Add forces and moments

Components are forward / right / down for force, and roll / pitch / yaw for moment. Additional moments use the model’s whole-canopy aerodynamic coefficients. Gravity and joint loads enter the body solve separately.
